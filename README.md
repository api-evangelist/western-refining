# Western Refining (western-refining)

Western Refining was an independent crude oil refiner and marketer of refined petroleum products headquartered in El Paso, Texas. The company operated refineries in El Paso, Texas; Gallup, New Mexico; and St. Paul Park, Minnesota before being acquired by Tesoro Corporation in 2017 for approximately $6.4 billion. Western Refining's assets are now part of Marathon Petroleum via the Tesoro/Andeavor acquisition chain.
